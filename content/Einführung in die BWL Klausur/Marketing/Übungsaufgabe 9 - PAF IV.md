#### Optimaler Tortellinipreis 
$G=p*x(p)-K[x(p)]$
$G_T=p*(250-30p)-5,5(250-30p)$
$G_T=250p-30p^2-1375+165p$
$G'_T=250-60p+165$
$0=-60p+415$
$p=6,92 \ Euro$
$X(6,92)=250-30*6,92=42,4$
$G(6,92)=6,92*42,4-5,5*42,4=60,21$
#### Optimaler Salatpreis 
$G_S=p*(160-15p)-1,5(160-15p)$
$G_S=160p-15p^2-240+22,5p$
$G'_S=160-30p+22,5$
$0=-30p+188,5$
$p=6,08 \ Euro$
$X(6,08)=160-15*6,08=68,8$
$G(6,08)=6,08*68,8-1,5*68,8=315,1$
